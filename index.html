Mission Briefing

Test your knowledge of the cosmos! You are a cadet preparing for launch: answer a series of space trivia questions, get instant feedback, and prove you're ready for the mission.

  • Click an answer to lock it in
  • Correct answers turn green, incorrect answers turn red
  • You have 3 lives — three wrong answers ends the mission
  • Click "Next Question" to continue
  • Answer every question with a life left to complete the mission
